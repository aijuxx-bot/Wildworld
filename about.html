Who We Are

Voices for the Voiceless

Founded in 2020, Wildworld began with a simple yet powerful conviction: Every animal deserves to be known. What started as a small team of passionate field biologists in Kenya has now grown into a global coalition of scientists, conservationists, and everyday guardians.

We don't just monitor wildlife; we actively protect them. Through satellite telemetry, habitat restoration, and local community empowerment, we build permanent shields around earth's most vulnerable creatures.
