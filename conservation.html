Our Journey

Conservation Timeline

Real action creates real impact. Track our major breakthroughs over the years as we fight on the frontlines to save endangered species and rebuild their vanishing worlds.

2020

The Genesis Project

Wildworld was founded with a mission to protect African plains. We successfully secured our first 50,000 acres of anti-poaching sanctuary in Kenya.

2022

Guardians of Ocean Noise

Launched the 'Echoes of Whales' project. Deployed 45 underwater sound monitors to map and establish marine noise-free shipping corridors.

2024

Leopard Trackers Deployed

Equipped critically endangered Amur Leopards with satellite tracking collars, increasing poacher detection accuracy by 40% in Eastern Russia.

2026

Global Sky Corridor

Established a multi-national avian flightway sanctuary spanning 12 borders, protecting migratory paths for millions of avian species.
